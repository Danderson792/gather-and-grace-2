Classic Sage and Onion Stuffing

Baked in a dish rather than inside the bird, this stuffing gets a crisp, buttery top and a soft, savory middle (and is easier to cook safely).

Prep time30 min
Cook time55 min
Serves12 servings

      Variations

      - Sausage: Brown 1 lb (450 g) pork sausage, crumbled, before the vegetables; cook the vegetables in the fat, reducing the butter by half. - Apple and pecan: Add 2 diced tart apples with the celery and fold in 1 cup toasted chopped pecans. - Mushroom: Add 12 oz (340 g) sliced mushrooms to the skillet after the onions soften and cook until browned.
