      ✳ A little kitchen wisdom

      Fixes

      - Lumpy? Blend with an immersion blender or pour through a fine sieve. - Too thin? Whisk 1 tablespoon cornstarch into 2 tablespoons cold water and stir into the simmering gravy. - Too thick? Whisk in warm stock a little at a time. - Too salty? Add unsalted stock, or a splash of cream.
